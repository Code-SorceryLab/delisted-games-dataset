Classification methods

MethodTitlesHow the labels were assigned
Manually reviewed A random sample, stratified by source, labelled by hand by the authors from rule suggestions and annotator notes.
LLM (Claude Haiku) Titles with free text (Dead Game Wiki notes, Delisted Games narratives) or records from several sources. Claude Haiku labelled them from the source data; they are not individually reviewed. On the manually reviewed pilot, Haiku agreed with the human labels on 97–99% of titles per dimension. Each title carries a high, medium or low confidence.
Rules Fixed rules over structured source fields, e.g. Steam Tracker type “Purchase disabled” → D1 = Unavailable. These titles have no free text; dimensions the fields do not cover stay Unknown.
